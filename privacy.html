Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information the StarNet website collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

1. Who we are

StarNet is a cybersecurity technology company founded by Jeko Abuladze. In this policy, "StarNet", "we" and "us" mean the operator of this website. You can reach us at starnetdev20@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Messages you send us. The contact form asks for your name, email address and message. The form does not send data to a StarNet server. It opens your own email app with the message filled in, and your email provider delivers it to us when you press send. We receive only what you choose to send.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, the hosting provider that serves these pages may record standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request. We use this only to keep the site running and secure.
  • Cookies and tracking. This website does not set cookies, does not use advertising trackers and does not run analytics.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts. The site's typefaces are loaded from Google's servers. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting and email providers. They process data on our behalf to deliver the site and your messages.
  • VMAXweb. The VMAXweb scanner runs on its own website, linked from our Products section. It has its own terms and privacy practices, and this policy does not cover it.

4. How we use information

We use the information above to answer your enquiries, to operate and secure the website, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

5. Legal basis

Where laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation apply, we process your message because you asked us to contact you (consent and steps taken at your request), and we process technical data because we have a legitimate interest in running a secure website.

6. How long we keep information

We keep emails only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any follow-up, and then delete them unless the law requires us to keep them. Server logs are kept by the hosting provider according to its own retention schedule.

7. Security

Security is our business, and we apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Reporting a vulnerability. If you find a security issue on this website, please email starnetdev20@gmail.com with the details. Please do not access, change or share other people's data while testing, and give us reasonable time to fix the problem before making it public.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us for access to your personal data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us and we will reply within a reasonable time.

9. International transfers

Our hosting, font and email providers may process data in countries other than your own. When that happens, the data is handled under the safeguards those providers offer.

10. Children

This website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our products change. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: starnetdev20@gmail.com.
